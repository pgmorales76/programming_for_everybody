# Welcome to ***Programming for Everybody***

This repo is dedicated to the, online, courses, by Charles Severance (aka Dr. Chuck).

In the various folders, you'll find each course.

The entire corpus, of Dr. Chuck's courses, may be found [at this link.](https://online.dr-chuck.com/).

I hope you find these courses, as interesting, and, as helpful, as I have.

I recommend taking Dr. Chuck's courses! It has been a tremendous help, to me, in learning programming!

Before you know it, you'll be well on your way, to becoming a master programmer!
